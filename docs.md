## What is uncloud?

uncloud is a plug-and-play home server that sits on your shelf and does exactly what Google Photos, iCloud, and Dropbox do — except your data **never leaves your home**. No monthly bills. No surveillance. No price hikes.

Once set up, your phone's photos and files automatically sync to the device over your home Wi-Fi. You can access everything remotely from anywhere in the world via the uncloud app, just like a regular cloud service.

> 💡 **The core idea:** The cloud is just someone else's computer. uncloud makes it *your* computer.

### Who is it for?

Anyone paying a monthly fee for Google One, iCloud+, Dropbox, or OneDrive — and would rather pay once and own their storage forever. No technical knowledge required. If you can plug in a router, you can set up uncloud.

## Setup Guide

The whole process takes under 10 minutes.

### Step 1 — Plug the device into your router

Use the included ethernet cable to connect uncloud to any available LAN port on your home router. Then plug in the USB-C power adapter. The device will power on and configure itself automatically — the **LED will blink teal** while it boots.

*~1 minute*

### Step 2 — Wait for the solid teal LED

Once the LED stops blinking and holds a steady teal glow, the device is online and ready. This usually takes 60–90 seconds on first boot while it sets up secure certificates and your private network address.

*~90 seconds*

### Step 3 — Download the app and scan the QR code

Install the **uncloud app** from the App Store or Google Play. Open the app, tap **Set up a device**, and point your camera at the QR code printed on the bottom of the uncloud unit. The app and device will pair instantly.

*Available on iOS & Android*

### Step 4 — Everything starts syncing

Grant the app access to your photo library when prompted. Your photos will begin backing up immediately over Wi-Fi. New photos sync automatically in the background from now on — nothing else to configure, ever.

> ⚠️ **First-time backup may take a while.** If you have thousands of photos, the initial sync can take several hours. Keep the app open and connected to Wi-Fi. Subsequent syncs are instant.

## FAQ

### Do I need any technical knowledge to set this up?

Not at all. If you can plug in a phone charger and scan a QR code, you can set up uncloud. The entire setup is designed to take under 10 minutes with zero technical background. No command line, no configuration files, no IT knowledge required.

---

### What happens if my internet goes down?

Your files are still fully accessible on your local home network — at full speed, with no limitations. uncloud runs entirely on your local network and doesn't require an internet connection for local access. Only remote access (from outside your home) requires internet.

---

### Is my data safe and encrypted?

Yes. All data stored on the device is encrypted at rest using **AES-256**. All communication between the app and the device is encrypted in transit using **TLS 1.3**. Your encryption keys are generated on your device and never leave your home — not even we can access your files.

---

### Can multiple people in my family use it?

Yes. uncloud supports family sharing — up to **6 users** can connect their phones to the same device, each with their own private storage space. Each person's files are kept separate and private from the others. Add family members from the app's **Family** tab.

---

### What if the device gets damaged or stops working?

We recommend enabling the app's **Redundant Backup** feature, which creates a second copy of your most important files on an external USB drive (sold separately). In case of hardware failure, simply attach the USB drive to a new uncloud unit and your data is restored within minutes. Hardware replacements are covered under our **2-year warranty**.

---

### How does remote access work? Do I need to forward ports?

No port forwarding required. uncloud establishes an outbound encrypted tunnel through our relay servers when you're away from home. Your router stays completely closed — no inbound rules, no exposed ports, no public IP needed. The relay only passes encrypted packets and cannot read your data.

---

### How is this different from a NAS?

A NAS is a great product for tech enthusiasts — but it requires significant setup, ongoing configuration, and technical know-how. uncloud is the consumer-friendly version: pre-configured, app-driven, and designed to just work for anyone. No RAID setup, no drive formatting, no SSH required.
